Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

A few things to know before you contact us.

Can you guarantee mutual fund returns?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Returns are not guaranteed, and investment choices should reflect your goals, time horizon and risk tolerance.

How do I ask about your service fees?

Send an enquiry through WhatsApp with the service you need. The scope, documents and applicable fee should be confirmed before work begins.

Can you help organise business accounts?

We can discuss record organisation, bookkeeping support, cash-flow tracking and basic MIS needs. The exact scope depends on your business and the service agreed.

Is this website a live stock-tip or price service?

No. Market education content may be informational and can be delayed. Always verify current information from reliable sources before making financial decisions.

Can I send sensitive documents on WhatsApp?

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, full bank credentials or other highly sensitive information in an initial enquiry. Ask us about an appropriate document-sharing method first.

How do I get started?

Choose a service, use the WhatsApp enquiry link and briefly explain what you need. We can then clarify the next steps.
